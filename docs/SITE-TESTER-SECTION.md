# Tester section for the ogstudio.org OG Paper page (draft, not published)

Paste near the top of the OG Paper page (`ogstudio.org/paper/`). The site is deployed from
omegagiven-com; the owner publishes. Remove the section once the app is in production.

```html
<section id="testers">
  <h2>Become a tester</h2>
  <p>OG Paper for Android is in Google's closed test. We need 12 testers for 14 days
     before it can go public. Two minutes, free, and you get the app early.</p>
  <ol>
    <li><a href="https://groups.google.com/g/ogpaper-testers">Join the tester group</a> with your Google account.</li>
    <li><a href="https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.ogpaper">Opt in to the test</a> with the same account.</li>
    <li><a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.ogpaper">Install OG Paper from Google Play</a>.</li>
    <li>Keep it installed for 14 days. Open it now and then.</li>
  </ol>
  <p><a class="button" href="https://groups.google.com/g/ogpaper-testers">Join the test</a></p>
</section>
```

Plain text version (for a Markdown page):

> **Become a tester.** OG Paper for Android needs 12 testers for 14 days before it can go public on Google Play.
> 1. Join the group: https://groups.google.com/g/ogpaper-testers
> 2. Opt in: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.ogpaper
> 3. Install: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.ogpaper
> 4. Keep it installed for 14 days.

The group must exist first, see "Before you post" in `LAUNCH-KIT.md`.
