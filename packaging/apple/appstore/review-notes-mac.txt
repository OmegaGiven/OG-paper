OG Paper is an infinite-canvas drawing app. No account or sign-in is needed, and it works fully offline.

To review:
1. Draw with the round tool button (bottom right) or the quick toolbar; zoom with pinch or the trackpad. There is no zoom limit.
2. Portals: open the tool button, pick Portal, tap "This view" in its panel, then drag a rectangle somewhere. The rectangle shows that view live; zoom into it to go through it.
3. Settings (gear, top right) has Pages, Export, Search, Bookmarks, the Timeline, UI options and Plugins.

Network features are optional and only used when the user chooses them: Share live (end-to-end encrypted, to a host or server the user picks) and Pages servers added by link. No data is collected; canvases stay on the device.

Why the app has the com.apple.security.network.server entitlement: Settings (gear) > Share live > "Host this canvas" makes this Mac the host of a live drawing session. The app then listens on a TCP port for incoming connections from the other people's OG Paper apps (desktop, mobile or web) that join with the link it shows, and relays everyone's drawing to them, end-to-end encrypted. Hosting starts only when the user presses that button and stops when they press Stop hosting. To test it: press Host this canvas, copy the link, and open it from another device or another copy of OG Paper on the same network (Settings > Share live > Join).

The binary no longer references _CGSSetWindowBackgroundBlurRadius (it came from the windowing library; that call has been removed).
