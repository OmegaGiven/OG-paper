OG Paper is an infinite-canvas drawing app. It works fully offline, and no account or sign-in is needed to use it.

New in this version: an optional OG Studio account keeps pages on our servers so they can be recovered on another device, with a copy kept on the device. A card offers this on first launch; tap Not now to dismiss it, and drawing is unaffected.

To test sign-in:
1. Tap Sign in on the card, or open Pages (gear, top right) and tap Sign in. The app opens ogstudio.org in the browser and shows a short code.
2. Sign in with the test account below. The page shows the same code. Tap Sign in, then return to the app, which finishes by itself.
3. In Pages, tap Keep this page. The page is listed in the account.
4. Delete my account, in the same panel, opens ogstudio.org to confirm.
