Zoom in forever.

OG Paper is a free, open-source infinite canvas. Sketch, write, paste pictures and tables — then dive in 1045 times deeper and keep drawing. Your work stays in a file you own.

Web · Windows · macOS · Linux · Android

Paper that never runs out

Draw at any size, anywhere. Only what is on screen is drawn, so it stays fast with millions of strokes.

Truly endless

No edges and no zoom limit: tested from 10-0.6 to 1049. Bookmark any view and fly back in one tap.

Ink, shapes, text & pictures

Pressure pen, marker, highlighter, hand-drawn shapes, 11 fonts (or your own), and paste in images, charts and spreadsheet tables.

Files you own

Every canvas is one open .ogp file with a published format. Works offline; nothing is locked in an account.
