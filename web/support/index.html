OG Paper

Support

Help with OG Paper, the free, open-source endless canvas.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/OmegaGiven/OG-paper/issues: describe what happened, your device and OS, and the app version (shown in the App Store, or the build in TestFlight). Feature ideas are welcome there too.

Getting started

  • Draw: pick a tool from the round button at the bottom right; its settings are in the tool panel.
  • Zoom: pinch, scroll or use the trackpad; there is no limit in or out.
  • Saved tools: keep tools with their settings in toolbars and the inventory (the numbered button on the quick bar).
  • Settings: the gear (top right) holds pages, files, export, search, bookmarks, the timeline, sharing, the UI options, plugins and hotkeys.
  • Try it first: the try-mode demo has a guided checklist, including the endless street.

Your files

Canvases are kept on your device. On macOS and iOS use Settings > Pages to see them, and Export or Save As to put a copy anywhere. The .ogp format is open and documented in the source repository.

Privacy

OG Paper collects no data. See the privacy policy.
