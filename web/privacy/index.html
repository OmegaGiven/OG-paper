OG Paper

Privacy policy

OG Paper (the apps for macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, Linux and Android, and the web app) · Updated October 30, 2026

The short version

The OG Paper apps collect no data about you. There are no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and nothing you draw is sent to us unless you choose to keep it in an account on omegagiven.com (below).

If you sign in to OG Paper on omegagiven.com, the pages you keep there are stored on our servers so you can open them on any device and share them. Nothing else changes: the apps themselves never connect to omegagiven.com.

Where your work is kept

Your canvases, bookmarks, saved tools, fonts, plugins and settings stay on your device: in the app's own storage (on macOS and iOS, the app's sandboxed container), in files you choose to save, or, in the web app, in your browser's storage. Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Sharing, only when you choose it

If you use Share live, Sync folder, a relay or a page server, your canvas travels only to the people, folders or servers you pick:

  • Live sharing connects to the host, relay or page server in the link you open or make. Live frames are encrypted end to end (ChaCha20-Poly1305) and changes are signed (Ed25519), so whoever carries them cannot read or alter them.
  • Sync folder writes a copy into a folder you pick; whatever syncs that folder (for example iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing) is governed by its own privacy terms.
  • Servers you or others host (og-paper --serve-dir) keep the pages shared with them.

Pages kept in an omegagiven.com account

OG Paper at omegagiven.com/paper/ can keep your pages in your omegagiven.com account. This is optional: signed out, it works like the web app above and keeps pages in your browser only. Signed in, and only for the pages you keep in the account:

  • What is stored: each page's content and history (strokes, text, pictures), its name and size, who it is shared with and how, and links you make for it. Pages are held on Cloudflare (Durable Objects, D1 and optionally R2), which runs the site.
  • Who can see a page: you, the people you share it with, and anyone you give a link to. Pages are never listed publicly. Live changes are encrypted in transit; the site keeps each page's keys so it can hand them to the people you share with, which means the site's administrators can open a page when they need to, for example to act on a report of abuse. They do not look at pages otherwise.
  • Your account: sign-in, your username and the email your sign-in provider gives are handled by omegagiven.com's own accounts. OG Paper uses them to show who is on a page and to let people share with you by username or email. Your IP address is used briefly for rate limits.
  • Storage limits: each account has a storage quota; we keep a count of how much your pages use.
  • Reports: if you report a page, we keep the report and your account id to review it.
  • Deleting: deleting a page deletes its content from our servers for everyone it was shared with. To delete everything, delete your pages (or ask us to, via the contact below).

Plugins

Plugins run sealed off: they cannot read your files, use the network or see anything but the canvas input the app hands them when you press their button.

Children

The OG Paper apps collect no personal information from anyone, children included. Accounts on omegagiven.com are governed by that site's terms.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: use the contact form or see the support page.
