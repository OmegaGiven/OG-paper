OG Paper

Privacy policy

OG Paper (the apps for macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, Linux and Android, and the web app) · Updated November 18, 2026

The short version

The OG Paper apps collect no data about you. There are no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and nothing you draw is sent to us unless you choose to keep it in an OG Studio account on ogstudio.org (below).

If you sign in with an OG Studio account, in the web app or in an app, the pages you keep in that account are stored on our servers so you can open them on any device and share them. Signed out, the apps never connect to ogstudio.org.

Where your work is kept

Your canvases, bookmarks, saved tools, fonts, plugins and settings stay on your device: in the app's own storage (on macOS and iOS, the app's sandboxed container), in files you choose to save, or, in the web app, in your browser's storage. Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Sharing, only when you choose it

If you use Share live, Sync folder, a relay or a page server, your canvas travels only to the people, folders or servers you pick:

  • Live sharing connects to the host, relay or page server in the link you open or make. Live frames are encrypted end to end (ChaCha20-Poly1305) and changes are signed (Ed25519), so whoever carries them cannot read or alter them.
  • Sync folder writes a copy into a folder you pick; whatever syncs that folder (for example iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing) is governed by its own privacy terms.
  • Servers you or others host (og-paper --serve-dir) keep the pages shared with them.

Pages kept in an OG Studio account

OG Paper at ogstudio.org/paper/ and the OG Paper apps can keep your pages in your OG Studio account. You sign in on ogstudio.org with Google, GitHub or Apple; the app never sees your password. This is optional: signed out, it works like the web app above and keeps pages in your browser only. Signed in, and only for the pages you keep in the account:

  • What is stored: each page's content and history (strokes, text, pictures), its name and size, who it is shared with and how, and links you make for it. Pages are held on Cloudflare (Durable Objects, D1 and optionally R2), which runs the site.
  • Who can see a page: you, the people you share it with, and anyone you give a link to. Pages are never listed publicly. Live changes are encrypted in transit; the site keeps each page's keys so it can hand them to the people you share with, which means the site's administrators can open a page when they need to, for example to act on a report of abuse. They do not look at pages otherwise.
  • Your account: sign-in, your username and the email your sign-in provider gives are handled by ogstudio.org's own accounts. OG Paper uses them to show who is on a page and to let people share with you by username or email. Your IP address is used briefly for rate limits.
  • Storage limits: each account has a storage quota; we keep a count of how much your pages use.
  • Reports: if you report a page, we keep the report and your account id to review it.
  • Deleting: deleting a page deletes its content from our servers for everyone it was shared with. To delete everything, see Deleting your OG Paper pages or account. Your pages go, except a page that has another owner, which stays with them.
  • Devices: a signed-in app keeps a sign-in token on your device. We keep which devices are signed in and when each was last used, and you can sign any of them out.

Deleting your OG Paper pages or account

You can delete single pages, or your whole OG Studio account with everything OG Paper keeps for it, at any time:

  1. One page: sign in to OG Paper at ogstudio.org/paper, open the Pages panel, pick the page and delete it. Its content and history are removed from our servers for everyone it was shared with.
  2. Your account: in OG Paper, open the Pages panel and choose Delete my account; or sign in at ogstudio.org/account and choose Delete account. This removes your account (username, email, sign-in ids), every page you own with its history and links, your signed-in devices, and your storage count. A page someone else owns stays with them, without you on it.
  3. Can't sign in? Use the contact form and we will delete the account for you.

Pages that never left your device are not on our servers; delete them in the app or remove the app.

Plugins

Plugins run sealed off: they cannot read your files, use the network or see anything but the canvas input the app hands them when you press their button.

Children

The OG Paper apps collect no personal information from anyone, children included. OG Studio accounts on ogstudio.org are covered by its privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: use the contact form or see the support page.
