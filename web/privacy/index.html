OG Paper

Privacy policy

OG Paper (the apps for macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, Linux and Android, and the web app) · Updated October 4, 2026

The short version

OG Paper collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and nothing you draw is sent to us. We do not run servers that see your canvases.

Where your work is kept

Your canvases, bookmarks, saved tools, fonts, plugins and settings stay on your device: in the app's own storage (on macOS and iOS, the app's sandboxed container), in files you choose to save, or, in the web app, in your browser's storage. Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Sharing, only when you choose it

If you use Share live, Sync folder, a relay or a page server, your canvas travels only to the people, folders or servers you pick:

  • Live sharing connects to the host, relay or page server in the link you open or make. Live frames are encrypted end to end (ChaCha20-Poly1305) and changes are signed (Ed25519), so whoever carries them cannot read or alter them.
  • Sync folder writes a copy into a folder you pick; whatever syncs that folder (for example iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing) is governed by its own privacy terms.
  • Servers you or others host (og-paper --serve-dir) keep the pages shared with them. The OG Paper project hosts none.

Plugins

Plugins run sealed off: they cannot read your files, use the network or see anything but the canvas input the app hands them when you press their button.

Children

OG Paper collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/OmegaGiven/OG-paper/issues.
